# NITI Aayog - Principles for Responsible AI (India)

In February 2021, NITI Aayog published "Approach Document for India Part 1 -
Principles for Responsible AI," derived from the Indian Constitution. It sets
out seven principles for responsible AI systems:

1. Safety and reliability
2. Equality
3. Inclusivity and non-discrimination
4. Privacy and security
5. Transparency
6. Accountability
7. Protection and reinforcement of positive human values

Unlike GDPR, this is guidance rather than binding law - India does not yet
have a standalone AI-specific statute. A second document (Part 2, August
2021) focuses on operationalizing these principles across government,
industry, and academia.

Source: NITI Aayog, "Approach Document for India Part 1 - Principles for
Responsible AI" (Feb 2021) and Part 2 (Aug 2021).
