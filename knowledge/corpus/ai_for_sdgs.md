# Responsible AI for Social Good and the SDGs

AI for Social Good refers to applying AI capabilities toward the United
Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and broader public benefit,
rather than purely commercial objectives.

Examples of application areas include:

- Healthcare access: diagnostic support tools in regions with a shortage of
  specialists, and readmission-risk models like the one this project audits,
  aimed at more efficient allocation of limited hospital resources.
- Climate and environment: satellite-image analysis for deforestation
  tracking, crop-yield prediction for food security, and energy-grid
  optimization.
- Education: adaptive learning systems that adjust to an individual
  student's pace, particularly relevant in under-resourced school systems.

A recurring criticism of "AI for Good" initiatives is that good intentions do
not eliminate the need for the same rigor applied elsewhere in this project -
bias audits, explainability, and human oversight - since a poorly-audited
system deployed for a social-good purpose can still cause real harm to the
population it intends to help.
