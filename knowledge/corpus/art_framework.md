# The ART Framework - Accountability, Responsibility, Transparency

ART is a shorthand used in AI ethics coursework for three related but
distinct properties a responsible AI system should have:

- Accountability: there must be a way to trace who is answerable for a
  decision an AI system made - e.g. logging which model version produced
  which prediction, so a specific decision can always be reviewed later.
- Responsibility: the people and organizations building and deploying the
  system take ownership of its real-world effects, including disclosing
  known limitations (e.g. via a model card) rather than hiding them.
- Transparency: the system's reasoning is inspectable - not just "trust the
  output," but a visible basis for it (e.g. an explanation of which inputs
  drove a specific prediction).

These three properties are about the SYSTEM's design, not about scoring any
individual answer's ethics - they are satisfied by how the system is built
and documented, not by a subjective judgment call.
