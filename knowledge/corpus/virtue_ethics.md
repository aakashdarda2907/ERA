# Virtue Ethics

Virtue ethics judges an action by what a person of good character (e.g. a
conscientious, careful professional) would do in that situation - focusing
on character and judgment rather than a fixed rule or a consequence
calculation.

Applied to AI systems, a virtue-ethics lens asks: would a careful,
conscientious practitioner (e.g. a clinician) treat this system's output as
one input among several, or blindly defer to it? It is inherently more
qualitative than utilitarian or deontological analysis, since it depends on
professional judgment rather than a computable metric.
