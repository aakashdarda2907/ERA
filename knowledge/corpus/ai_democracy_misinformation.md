# AI, Democracy, and Misinformation

AI systems, especially generative models, intersect with democratic
processes and public information in several ways:

- Synthetic content at scale: generative AI can produce large volumes of
  political messaging, fake reviews, or fabricated news content far more
  cheaply than human-written equivalents, straining existing fact-checking
  and content-moderation capacity.
- Microtargeting: AI-driven ad targeting can deliver different, sometimes
  contradictory messages to different audiences, reducing shared public
  discourse around a common set of facts or claims.
- Recommendation-system effects: engagement-optimized algorithms can amplify
  polarizing or sensational content, not because it is accurate, but because
  it drives more interaction.
- Deepfakes in political contexts: fabricated audio or video of public
  figures can spread before verification is possible, with real-world
  consequences even after being debunked.

Proposed mitigations include mandatory disclosure of AI-generated political
content, provenance watermarking, and platform policies requiring labeling of
synthetic media - though enforcement across borders and platforms remains
difficult.
