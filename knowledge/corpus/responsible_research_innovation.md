# Responsible Research and Innovation (RRI)

Responsible Research and Innovation is a framework for embedding ethical
and societal considerations throughout a technology's development process,
rather than treating ethics as a final compliance check after the system
is already built. A commonly used structure is the AREA framework:

- Anticipate: think ahead about intended and unintended consequences before
  building.
- Reflect: examine the assumptions, values, and purposes underlying the
  work as it progresses.
- Engage: involve affected stakeholders and the public in shaping the
  research or product, not just experts.
- Act: use the above to actually adjust the direction of the work, not
  just document concerns.

Applied to this project, RRI is the reason fairness auditing and
explainability were built into the model pipeline from the start (Anticipate
+ Reflect), rather than added as an afterthought once the model was already
deployed.
