# Indian AI Policy

India's approach to AI policy has developed through several key documents and
institutions rather than a single comprehensive law:

- NITI Aayog's National Strategy for AI (2018): identified five priority
  sectors for AI adoption - healthcare, agriculture, education, smart
  cities/infrastructure, and smart mobility/transportation.
- NITI Aayog's Principles for Responsible AI (2021): the ART-based guidance
  document covered elsewhere in this corpus.
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023): India's primary data
  protection law, broadly comparable in purpose to GDPR though with notable
  differences in scope, consent mechanisms, and enforcement structure.
- IndiaAI Mission: a government initiative to build domestic AI compute
  infrastructure, datasets, and skilling programs, aimed at reducing reliance
  on foreign AI infrastructure.

Unlike the EU, India does not yet have a single binding, AI-specific
regulatory statute - its approach currently combines sector-specific rules,
non-binding principles, and general data-protection law rather than one
comprehensive AI act.
