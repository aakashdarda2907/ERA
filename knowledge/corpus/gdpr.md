# GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

GDPR is the EU's data protection law. Article 5 sets out seven core principles
for processing personal data:

1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency - processing must be legal and not
   hidden from the person whose data it is.
2. Purpose limitation - data collected for one stated purpose should not be
   reused for an unrelated one.
3. Data minimisation - collect only what is actually necessary.
4. Accuracy - keep personal data correct and up to date.
5. Storage limitation - do not keep data longer than needed.
6. Integrity and confidentiality - keep data secure from unauthorized access.
7. Accountability - organizations must be able to demonstrate compliance,
   not just claim it.

Accountability is considered the overarching principle: it requires
documented evidence (audit trails, policies, records) that the other six
principles are actually being followed, not just written down.

Source: Article 5, GDPR; European Commission and Data Protection Commission
guidance summaries.
