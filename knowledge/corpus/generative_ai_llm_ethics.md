# Generative AI, LLM Ethics, and Deepfakes

Generative AI and large language models raise distinct ethical concerns
beyond traditional predictive AI:

- Hallucination: generating confident, fluent, but false or unverifiable
  content - which is why this project deliberately avoids free generation
  for factual claims, grounding every answer in cached data or a cited
  document instead.
- Deepfakes and synthetic media: AI-generated images, audio, or video that
  can convincingly impersonate real people, used for fraud, harassment, or
  disinformation.
- Misinformation at scale: generative models can produce large volumes of
  plausible-sounding false content very cheaply, straining fact-checking
  and content-moderation systems built for a lower-volume era.

Mitigation approaches include watermarking or embedding provenance metadata
in AI-generated content, detection classifiers trained to spot synthetic
media, and platform policies requiring disclosure when content is
AI-generated.
