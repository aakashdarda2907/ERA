# Human-Centered AI Design

Human-centered AI design means building systems around real human needs,
context, and limitations, rather than optimizing a metric in isolation.
Core ideas include:

- Keeping a human in the loop for consequential decisions, rather than
  fully automating them - the AI supports a decision, it does not replace
  the decision-maker.
- Designing for the actual context of use - a clinician glancing at a
  screen between patients needs different information than a data
  scientist auditing a model offline.
- Accessible, inclusive design - ensuring the system works for users with
  different abilities, literacy levels, and languages, not just the
  average case in the training data.
- Giving users meaningful control - the ability to question, override, or
  ignore an AI recommendation, not just receive it.

This is closely linked to explainability: a system cannot be genuinely
human-centered if the humans using it cannot understand why it produced a
given output.
